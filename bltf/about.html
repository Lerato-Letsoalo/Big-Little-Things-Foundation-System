Our Story

We Believe in the Power of Small Acts

Established in 2021 by Iman Cassiem, Chloe Grotewold and Lehlohonolo Mgedeza, Big Little Things Foundation began with a simple intention: to uplift those in need, starting with something as essential as sanitary care.

Join Our Team Get in Touch
How It All Began

A Small Idea That Grew Into a Movement

In 2021, we held our first outreach drive — distributing sanitary towels to young girls in Gauteng and the Free State. Our goal was modest, but the response was overwhelming. We exceeded our expectations threefold, and in doing so, realised the power of collective action and compassion.

This success sparked a broader vision. We saw the potential to impact not just girls and women, but communities across South Africa, especially children and youth in need of basic support and opportunities.

What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Purpose

Our Mission

To provide everyday essentials — clothing, food, and toiletries — to under-resourced communities throughout South Africa, while engaging young volunteers in every project to cultivate a culture of giving and civic responsibility.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate, empowered South Africa where every person — especially the youth — has the opportunity to thrive, contribute, and feel seen.

Our Purpose

In a fast-paced world where many young people feel isolated or disengaged, we strive to create space for purpose, connection, and meaningful impact — empowering the youth to uplift others as one of the most powerful ways to create lasting change.

What We Stand For
