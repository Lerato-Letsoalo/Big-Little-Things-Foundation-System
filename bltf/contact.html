FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a volunteer?

+
Simply fill in the registration form on our Volunteer page. Once submitted, your application will be reviewed within 48 hours and you'll receive a confirmation email with next steps.

Is there a minimum age to volunteer?

+
Volunteers must be at least 16 years old. Those under 18 will need written parental consent before participating in events.

How are donations used?

+
100% of donations go directly toward our projects — food parcels, school supplies, blankets, and event costs. Our admin is funded separately through grants. Financial reports are available on request.

Can I donate items instead of money?

+
Absolutely! We accept food, clothing, school supplies, sanitary products, and blankets. Contact us to arrange a drop-off at our Pretoria office or at one of our collection points.

How do I get my volunteer certificate?

+
After your hours are logged and approved by an admin, your certificate is automatically generated in your volunteer dashboard. You can download it as a PDF at any time.

How can my company partner with you?

+
We offer corporate volunteering days, CSI partnership packages, and co-branded campaigns. Email us at partnerships@biglittlethings.org.za and our team will arrange a meeting.
