Who We Are

Driven by Youth,
Powered by Compassion

Big Little Things Foundation was established in 2021 by Iman Cassiem, Chloe Grotewold and Lehlohonolo Mgedeza with a simple intention: to uplift those in need, starting with something as essential as sanitary care. Today we provide everyday essentials — clothing, food, and toiletries — to under-resourced communities across South Africa.

Youth involvement is a cornerstone of every project. By engaging young volunteers in each of our drives, we cultivate a culture of giving, civic responsibility, and meaningful connection.

The People Behind the Mission

Meet the Team

Our foundation is led by a passionate team of young change-makers dedicated to community transformation.

Iman Cassiem

Treasurer & Volunteer Coordinator

Iman Cassiem is a passionate community advocate who believes that meaningful change often starts with the little things we do for others. As the Treasurer & Volunteer Coordinator at Big Little Things Foundation, she helps oversee the organisation’s finances, coordinate volunteers and ensure that Big Little Things’ initiatives are organised, impactful and sustainable. With a background in Accounting Science and a strong interest in leadership, community development and youth empowerment, Iman brings her practical skills and dedication into ensuring that Big Little Things is run ethically and functions efficiently. She is passionate about creating opportunities for people to get involved, supporting communities in need and inspiring others to recognise that even the smallest contribution can make a lasting difference.

072 516 9605

Chloe Grotewold

Secretary & Donor Sourcing

Chloe Grotewold is a dedicated community advocate and Secretary at Big Little Things Foundation, where she supports the organisation’s administration, donor relationships and fundraising initiatives. She is passionate about creating meaningful opportunities to give back and connecting individuals and businesses with causes where their contributions can make a tangible difference. Based in Johannesburg, Chloe leads Big Little Things’ East Rand and Johannesburg outreach drives, coordinating four community initiatives each year and managing local donation efforts. Through her work, she is committed to strengthening community partnerships, encouraging generosity and helping turn small contributions into meaningful impact.

078 162 6965

Hloni Mgedeza

Marketing & Communications

Hloni Mgedeza is a passionate creative and community advocate who believes that small acts of kindness can make a big difference. As the Marketing & Communications director at Big Little Things Foundation, she helps share BLT’s story, connect with communities and create awareness around its initiatives. With a background in Somatology, beauty and content creation, Hloni brings her creativity and love for people into her work. She is passionate about giving back, creating meaningful experiences and inspiring others to see the impact that even the little things can make.
